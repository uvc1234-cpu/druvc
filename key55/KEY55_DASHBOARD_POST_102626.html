My read. The money that is still working is in energy, the defensive sectors and the dollar. Growth, metals and most of the world outside the US are red on every clock. A market that is negative across the board but green in its defensive corners is a little-or-no-exposure reading, with what exposure there is held where the lights are green.

This is one close. Every fund counts equally, so a single commodity fund weighs as much as SPY. The groups are small (four to eleven names), so one fund changing color moves a group's count by 10 to 25 points.
